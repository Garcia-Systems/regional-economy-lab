# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and attentive to educational accessibility. Harassment, insults, sexualized attention, threats, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards and may remove contributions or temporarily or permanently ban participants for inappropriate behavior. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub repository-owner contact tools. Maintainers will respect reporters' privacy.
