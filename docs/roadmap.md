# Roadmap

Chapters 0–20 are implemented. They cover the deterministic monthly engine, canonical transaction stages and accounting classifications, subsystem reports, dashboards, decision evidence, shocks, an educational resilience summary, deterministic twelve-month seasonal aggregation, and validated custom-region YAML. The maintained [chapter map](chapter-map.md) and [debugging guide](debugging.md) describe the executable curriculum.

Implemented does not mean fully integrated in every economic sense. University and healthcare payroll/funding remain descriptive where documented; housing and workforce expose aggregate capacity indicators rather than dynamic migration or production feedback; deposits are stocks and credit is capacity; regional sources and uses remain not yet consolidated. Annual profiles invoke twelve independently configured monthly runs and do not carry deposits, reserves, savings, or inventory balances forward.

## Genuinely future research directions

Potential future work—none promised or represented as present behavior—includes probabilistic simulation, forecasting, optimization, real public-data adapters with provenance, agent-based modeling, richer state carry-forward, and web interfaces. Release preparation, packaging cleanup, and CI changes are deliberately outside this curriculum milestone.
